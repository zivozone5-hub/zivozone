# ZIVOZONE V1228 — Growth Action Plan

## 1) Measurement
GA4 events:
- `story_completed`
- `share_result`
- `daily_challenge_open`
- `daily_challenge_completed`
- SPA `page_view`

Primary funnel:
Landing → Room/Game → Start → Complete → Share → Return/second session.

Recommended GA4 explorations:
- Engagement by `room_id` / `game_id`
- Story completion rate
- Daily challenge open → completion
- Share rate = share_result / completed
- Organic landing pages by Search Console

## 2) SEO
Do not use `meta keywords`; place search intent naturally in:
- title
- meta description
- H1
- first paragraph
- internal links
- visible room/category copy
- structured data where appropriate

Target clusters:
1. ألعاب ذكاء أونلاين
2. ألعاب ألغاز أونلاين
3. ألعاب رياضيات ممتعة
4. ألغاز منطق صعبة
5. ألعاب ذاكرة وتركيز
6. اختبار ذكاء سريع
7. ألعاب كرة قدم تفاعلية
8. تحديات كرة القدم
9. ألعاب رعب تفاعلية
10. قصص رعب تفاعلية
11. ألعاب تحقيق وألغاز بوليسية
12. ألعاب تفكير بدون تحميل
13. تحدي ذكاء يومي
14. online brain games
15. interactive puzzle games

## 3) 30-day publishing cadence
Week 1:
- 3 puzzle pages
- 2 football challenge pages
- 2 interactive horror/story pages
- 1 daily challenge landing page

Week 2:
- Publish 5 supporting articles/landing pages around the strongest Search Console queries.
- Add 3–5 internal links from each page to relevant games.

Week 3:
- Turn top-performing games into short-form videos.
- Publish one result challenge per day.
- Add one share CTA after completion.

Week 4:
- Refresh titles/descriptions for pages with impressions but low CTR.
- Expand pages with clicks but low engagement.
- Remove/merge near-duplicate pages.

## 4) Social loop
Every completed challenge:
1. Show score.
2. Show one-tap Share.
3. Give a direct clean URL.
4. Ask the viewer to beat the score.
5. Store campaign attribution in URL parameters.

Example:
`/challenges/logic?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=score_challenge`

Use campaign parameters for measurement; keep canonical URLs clean without UTM parameters.

## 5) KPI targets
Track trends rather than vanity totals:
- Organic impressions
- Organic clicks
- CTR
- engaged sessions
- challenge completion rate
- share rate
- returning users
- indexed story/game URLs
- pages receiving organic traffic

Review weekly; do not optimize on a single day's data.
