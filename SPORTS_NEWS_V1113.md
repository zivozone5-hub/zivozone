# ZIVOZONE V1113 — SPORTS NEWS CORE

- Real sports headlines are ingested into `data/news.json`.
- GitHub Actions updates the feed hourly for free.
- Sources: public RSS feeds including Arabic Google News topic feeds plus BBC Sport/ESPN where available.
- No paid API, no Firebase Function, and no browser scraping.
- Only headline/source/date/link are stored; the article remains on its original site.
